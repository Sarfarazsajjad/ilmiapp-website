Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: 6 October 2026. Your privacy and safety are paramount. Read our commitment to protecting student information.

Summary

  • ilmi is a child-directed educational app designed to help children learn through lessons, stories, tracing activities, games, quizzes, and interactive exercises.
  • This Privacy Policy explains what information ilmi collects, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to parents, guardians, schools, and other users.
  • ilmi is designed with children's privacy in mind. We do not sell personal information, do not create advertising profiles, and do not use children's information for behavioural advertising.
  • If you purchase a subscription or in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

1. Who We Are

  • ilmi is a learning application that teaches early-primary children through lessons, stories, tracing games, quizzes, and other interactive learning activities.
  • The app may provide learning content in Urdu, Sindhi, English, and Punjabi.
  • Learning progress can be synchronized with our secure backend servers so that a child can continue learning when using another supported device.

2. Information We Collect

  • We collect only the information needed to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the ilmi service.

Child Information

    When a child profile is created with appropriate parental consent, we may collect:

  • Name or nickname — to display the child's profile and personalize the learning experience.
  • Age — to appropriately organize the child's learning journey.
  • Cartoon animal avatar — to help the child recognize their profile.
  • Learning progress — including course, level, section, lesson and activity information.
  • Results — including completion status, pass/fail status, scores, correct and total actions, and accuracy.
  • Date progress was saved — we store the day on which progress was saved, not the time of day.

    • For children registered through a school or organization, the school may also provide information such as:

  • Roll number.
  • Grade.

    • The parent account flow in the app does not request all of these school-specific fields.

    We do not intentionally collect a child's:

  • Precise location
  • Photograph
  • Voice recordings
  • Contacts
  • Camera or microphone data

    • Parent or Guardian Information

    Parent sign-in is handled through Google Sign-In using Firebase Authentication on Android.

    We may receive and store:

  • Parent's email address
  • Parent's name, if available through the Google account
  • Firebase user ID
  • Time of the parent's last sign-in
  • Purchase and subscription status
  • We never receive or store your Google password
  • The iOS version of ilmi is built separately and may use Sign in with Apple for authentication.
  • Your Google or Apple account remains controlled by the respective provider. Deleting your ilmi account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

3. Verifiable Parental Consent

  • Because ilmi is designed for children, we obtain parental or guardian consent before collecting and storing personal information about a child.
  • After signing in, the parent or guardian is shown a consent screen before child information is collected.

    • The parent or guardian must confirm that they are the child's parent or legal guardian and consent to the collection and storage of:

  • Child's name
  • Child's age
  • Child's avatar
  • Child's learning progress
  • Consent is requested from every parent or guardian regardless of the child's age.
  • If consent is declined, the user is signed out and information about the child is not sent to our servers.
  • If unsynchronized learning data needs to be retained temporarily on the device before sign-out, it may be kept for a limited period as described in our retention section and is deleted if the user does not sign in again within that period.

    • We record:

  • Consent version
  • Consent method
  • Date and time consent was accepted
  • Parent account associated with the consent
  • The consent record applies to the parent account and covers the child profiles associated with that account.
  • If consent is accepted while the device is offline, the consent information remains temporarily on the device and is uploaded when the device reconnects.
  • Parents or guardians may withdraw consent by deleting their ilmi account.

4. Learning Progress

    For each child, ilmi may store:

  • Level
  • Section
  • Lesson
  • Activity
  • Completion status
  • Accuracy
  • Date on which progress was saved
  • We do not store how long a child spends on an individual lesson or activity.

    • Learning progress is used to:

  • Provide the learning service
  • Save and restore progress
  • Allow learning to continue across supported devices
  • Provide progress information to authorized school staff where school mode is used
  • Allow our authorized staff to provide support and administer the service
  • We do not use learning progress to create behavioural advertising profiles.

5. School and Organization Mode

  • School or organization mode is available on Android
  • A school or organization may have its own account and may add students and issue devices for educational use.
  • School staff may sign in using an organization account. Organization passwords are stored using salted password hashes.

    • For students belonging to a school, authorized school staff may access information belonging to their own students, including:

  • Name
  • Roll number
  • Grade
  • Avatar
  • Learning progress
  • Day of last synchronization
  • Schools cannot access information belonging to students of other schools.
  • Our authorized staff may access child profile and progress information when necessary for support, administration, security, or operation of the service.
  • The school is responsible for obtaining any authorization or consent required for the students it registers.
  • Parents of school-registered children may contact their school or privacy@ilmiapp.com to request review, correction, export, or deletion of their child's information.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed through the applicable app store

    • Android

  • Payments are processed through Google Play Billing.
  • We do not receive or store your full payment card information.

    • Google Play may provide us with information necessary to verify and manage your purchase, including:

  • Product ID
  • Package name
  • Purchase token
  • Subscription state
  • Start date
  • Expiry date
  • We use this information to activate, restore, and manage premium features.

    • iOS

  • Purchases are processed through the Apple App Store / Apple's in-app purchase system.
  • We do not receive or store your full payment card information.
  • Purchase information is used only as necessary to provide and manage purchased features.
  • No child learning information is included in purchase records.

7. Crash Reports

  • Production Android versions of ilmi use Google Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and resolve application crashes and improve stability.

    • Crash reports may contain:

  • Error type
  • Error message
  • Code location where the error occurred
  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • A Firebase installation identifier
  • We take measures to remove emails, UUIDs, and long ID-like strings from error messages before they are sent.

    • We do not intentionally include:

  • Child names
  • Parent names
  • School identifiers
  • Child profile identifiers
  • Request contents
  • We cannot guarantee that a free-text error message will never contain personal information, but we design our application to use error types and codes rather than personal information.
  • Crashes that occur before application startup is completed may not be reported.
  • The iOS version of ilmi does not send crash reports.

8. Performance Monitoring

  • Production Android versions of ilmi use Google Firebase to measure the performance of limited technical operations.

    • This includes measuring:

  • How long the local database takes to open
  • How long course content takes to load
  • How long certain server requests take

    • Server requests are recorded using the request method and route template, such as:

  • POST /progress_sync/profile
  • Identifiers are replaced with placeholders such as \:id.

    • We do not intentionally include:

  • Names
  • Query strings
  • Lesson content
  • Child information
  • Request contents
  • Firebase Performance may also receive standard technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, and a Firebase installation identifier.
  • Performance monitoring is not used to track individual screens, lessons, children, or behavioural usage.

9. Analytics and Behavioural Tracking

  • ilmi does not use general analytics or behavioural tracking.

    • We do not intentionally collect or record:

  • Screens visited
  • Usage events
  • Sessions
  • Behavioural profiles
  • Advertising profiles
  • How frequently a particular child uses the app
  • How long a child uses the app
  • We do not use learning data for behavioural advertising.

10. Device Information and Identifiers

  • Certain technical services, such as Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring on Android, may receive standard technical information including:
  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Firebase installation identifier

    • We do not intentionally collect or use:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • Apple IDFA
  • Android ID for tracking
  • Precise device location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Voice recordings
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions.

11. Advertising

    iOS

  • ilmi does not show advertisements on iOS.

    • Android

  • ilmi may display advertisements between lessons to users who do not have an active subscription.
  • Advertisements on Android are:
  • Child-directed
  • Non-personalized
  • Configured using Google's child-directed and under-age settings
  • Restricted to the strictest applicable content rating
  • Served without using an advertising ID
  • Not based on behavioural tracking
  • Not based on a child's learning profile
  • We do not send a child's personal information to advertising networks for advertising profiles.
  • Ads are not displayed in school or organization mode.
  • Where advertising is enabled, ilmi may use Google AdMob to serve advertisements and measure basic advertising performance.
  • We do not sell personal information or create advertising profiles.

12. IP Addresses

  • Our application servers do not store IP addresses in our database or application logs.
  • For security purposes, such as reducing password-guessing and abuse attempts, our server may temporarily hold a connecting address in memory for approximately one to fifteen minutes before it is discarded.
  • Our hosting provider or network infrastructure may technically process an IP address to deliver the connection. We do not use IP addresses to identify or locate users.

13. How We Use Information

  • We use information to:
  • Provide and maintain ilmi
  • Create and manage authorized user profiles
  • Provide personalized learning experiences
  • Save and synchronize learning progress
  • Restore learning progress across supported devices
  • Provide school and organization functionality
  • Process and manage subscriptions
  • Restore purchased features
  • Diagnose application crashes
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Prevent abuse and protect service security
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and our service
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use children's information to build behavioural advertising profiles.

15. Data Storage and Retention

  • We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service or as otherwise required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Child profile and learning progress Device and servers Until the child profile or account is deleted

  • Parents do not receive an ilmi refresh token. Authentication is managed through Google Firebase or the applicable authentication provider.

    • For account emails sent to school and staff users, we may retain limited email delivery information such as:

  • Recipient address
  • Type of email
  • Whether the email was sent
  • Error code, if any
  • Time of sending
  • We do not retain the contents of those emails, links, or passwords.
  • Server logs do not intentionally contain children's names, learning progress, or stored IP addresses.

16. Data Security

  • We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information.
  • These measures include:
  • HTTPS encryption for traffic between the app and our servers
  • Signed and expiring authentication tokens
  • Access controls limiting users to information they are authorized to access
  • School access restricted to the school's own students
  • Salted password hashes for school and staff accounts
  • Secure operating-system storage for applicable device tokens
  • Exclusion of child data from operating-system cloud backup
  • Ability to revoke sessions
  • No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.
  • If a security incident affects children's personal information, we will take appropriate action and notify affected individuals and regulators where required by applicable law.

17. Your Rights and Choices

  • Parents and guardians can manage their child's information through the app.
  • Depending on the account and profile type, you may be able to:
  • Review your child's name
  • Correct your child's name
  • Correct your child's age
  • Change the child's avatar
  • Review learning results
  • Delete an individual child profile
  • Delete the entire ilmi account
  • Withdraw parental consent by deleting the account
  • Request access, correction, export, or deletion of school-managed child information

18. Deleting Your Account

  • You can delete your account through:
  • Settings → Delete Account
  • An adult verification step is required.
  • Deleting your account permanently deletes, from our servers, where applicable:
  • Your ilmi account
  • Child profiles
  • Learning progress
  • Consent records
  • Firebase Authentication sign-in record
  • The app also clears applicable data stored on the device.
  • An internet connection is required for account deletion to be completed on our servers.
  • Deleting your ilmi account does not delete your Google or Apple account.
  • Copies of information contained in backups maintained by our hosting provider may remain temporarily until those backups expire.

19. School-Managed Profiles

  • For children registered through a school, the school controls the student record.
  • The school administrator can delete an individual student or the organization's student data.
  • Parents of school-registered children may also contact:
  • info@itretina.com
  • to request review, correction, export, or deletion of their child's information.
  • We aim to respond to such requests within 30 days.

20. Children's Privacy

  • ilmi is designed for children and is intended to be used with parental, guardian, or school supervision.
  • We obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child through the parent flow.
  • We collect only information reasonably necessary to provide the educational service.
  • Children cannot independently:
  • Make purchases
  • Change protected settings
  • Delete account data
  • without the applicable adult verification process.
  • We do not intentionally collect a child's:
  • Voice
  • Photograph
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Camera or microphone data
  • If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us in a way that was not intended or authorized, please contact us at:
  • info@itretina.com
  • We will review the request and delete information where required by applicable law.

21. Offline Use and Synchronization

  • ilmi's learning content is designed to support offline learning after the required initial setup.
  • Depending on the account and features being used, an internet connection may be required for:
  • Sign-in
  • Creating or accessing an account
  • Parental consent synchronization
  • Initial setup
  • Synchronizing learning progress
  • Restoring progress on another device
  • Subscription verification
  • Account deletion
  • Lessons, stories, games, tracing activities, and other downloaded learning content can continue to work offline where supported.
  • When an internet connection becomes available, applicable learning progress can synchronize with our secure backend.

22. Information You Provide to Support

  • If you contact us directly, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide in your support request.
  • We use this information only to:
  • Respond to your request
  • Investigate technical issues
  • Provide customer support
  • Address privacy or deletion requests
  • Protect the security of the service
  • If a child has provided personal information directly through a support interaction, please notify us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

23. Your Privacy Choices

  • You may:
  • Manage applicable device privacy settings
  • Control available advertising and privacy settings through your device
  • Choose whether to use premium features
  • Delete child profiles
  • Delete your ilmi account
  • Request deletion of information submitted directly through support
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns
  • Uninstalling the app generally removes locally stored app data from the device, subject to the operating system's behavior. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete server-side account information. To delete server-side account information, use Settings → Delete Account or contact us where applicable.

24. International and Regional Privacy Rights

  • We aim to comply with applicable privacy and children's data protection requirements.
  • If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom and believe that your personal information has been handled unlawfully, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with your applicable data protection authority.
  • Parents and guardians may also contact us directly regarding privacy requests.

25. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
  • When we make changes, we will update the Last updated date at the beginning of this policy.
  • If a change materially affects what information we collect or how we use it, we will take appropriate steps to obtain consent again where required.
  • If you do not agree with an updated policy, you may stop using ilmi and delete your account.
