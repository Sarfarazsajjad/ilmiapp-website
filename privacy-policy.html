Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: February 18, 2026. Your privacy and safety are paramount. Read our commitment to protecting student information.

1. Summary

  • ilmi’s learning content and premium features work locally on your device.
  • We do not require account creation to use Ilmi.
  • If you enable ads, we may show ads using third-party advertising services (e.g., Google AdMob).
  • If you purchase a subscription or in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: Optional in-app profiles (e.g., child profile name/nickname/grade). This data is stored on your device unless you contact us and explicitly share information with support.
  • Information collected automatically:Depending on your device settings and app configuration, the app and its third-party services may collect device information (device model, OS version, language, app version), approximate location (coarse location derived from IP for ad serving, if ads are enabled), and diagnostics (crash logs, performance data).
    We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as CNIC, precise location, or financial details).
🛡️
Strict Zero-Ad Guarantee: Ilmi never displays third-party advertisements, never builds behavioral advertising profiles, and never sells user data to third parties.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the app.
  • Improve stability and performance (e.g., crash diagnostics).
  • Show ads (if enabled) and measure ad performance.
  • Process subscriptions/in-app purchases through the app stores
  • Respond to your support requests

4. Advertising (AdMob) and Analytics

If Ilmi shows ads, we may use Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data to display ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Payments are processed by Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The stores may provide us limited information (e.g., purchase status) to unlock premium features.

6. Children’s Privacy

ilmi is designed for learning and may be used by children with parental/guardian supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to operate the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly (e.g., via support), contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Storage and Retention

An internet connection is required only for the initial Google Sign-In, profile creation, and first-time setup. After that, all lessons, stories, games, and learning activities work completely offline. Student progress is automatically saved on the device and seamlessly synchronized with secure backend servers whenever an internet connection becomes available.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers (e.g., ad networks, crash reporting) only as needed, or if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

9. Your Choices

  • Control ad personalization and privacy permissions from your device settings.
  • Remove on-device data by uninstalling the app (clears locally stored app data on most devices).
  • Request deletion of any information you sent via support by contacting us.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
