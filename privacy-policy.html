Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: August 4, 2026. Your privacy and safety are paramount. Read our commitment to protecting student information.

1. Our Commitment to Student Safety

At IlmiApp.com ("Ilmi", "we", "us", or "our"), we believe that learning environments must be completely safe, respectful, and private. We adhere to the highest global educational data standards, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

To deliver our adaptive learning experiences, we collect minimal personal data:

  • Account Details: Student display name, grade level, and parent/guardian email address.
  • Learning Progress Metrics: Quiz responses, study session timestamps, accuracy rates, and streak records used exclusively to calibrate adaptive questions.
  • Technical Data: Device model, OS version, and app crash logs for technical maintenance.
🛡️
Strict Zero-Ad Guarantee: Ilmi never displays third-party advertisements, never builds behavioral advertising profiles, and never sells user data to third parties.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely for educational purposes:

  • Personalizing the adaptive difficulty engine to match student learning speed.
  • Generating weekly progress summaries for parents and linked educators.
  • Improving curriculum quality and fixing technical bugs in the software.

4. Parental & Student Rights

Parents and legal guardians have complete control over their child's data at all times:

  • Right to Review: Inspect all stored learning records associated with your account.
  • Right to Delete: Request immediate, permanent deletion of your child's profile and data.
  • Right to Export: Export learning achievements and metrics in portable formats.

5. Data Security Standards

All communications between the Ilmi app and servers are protected using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Data stored on servers is encrypted at rest using AES-256 bit protocols. Regular third-party security audits ensure robust defense against unauthorized access.

6. Contacting Our Privacy Team

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or student data handling, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

Email: privacy@ilmiapp.com
Address: Ilmi Education Technologies Inc., Privacy & Legal Operations, San Francisco, CA.
