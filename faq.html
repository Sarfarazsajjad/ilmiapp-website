Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about Ilmi's learning adventure, pricing, curriculum, and safety.

🔍

Ilmi is an interactive educational application for kids and students. Guided by friendly animal mentors, kids learn math, science, reading, and coding through 15-minute daily quests built on active recall and spaced repetition science.

Yes! Ilmi offers a Free Forever Tier including access to daily learning quests, core flashcards, and basic progress metrics. Ilmi Premium unlocks unlimited offline downloads, advanced skill tree analytics, multi-profile family sharing, and test prep modules.

Our algorithm measures accuracy and response speed. If a child answers quickly, higher-order challenges unlock. If they struggle, animal mentors step in with step-by-step visual hints to keep learning positive and stress-free.

Ilmi is available on iOS (iPhones & iPads with iOS 14.0+) and Android (smartphones & tablets with Android 8.0+). You can also access study decks via modern web browsers.

Yes! Students can download up to 5 study units for offline practice during travel. Progress and streaks automatically sync to the cloud once reconnected to Wi-Fi.

Child safety is our highest promise. Ilmi is 100% ad-free, COPPA compliant, and GDPR compliant. We never sell student data or track kids across external websites. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Parents can link accounts in the Parent Portal to view weekly progress reports, active streaks, completed skill nodes, and customize study time caps.

Ilmi covers K-12 topics including Mathematics, Natural Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Computer Science & AI, Humanities, and Standardized Test Prep. See our Curriculum Treehouse.
