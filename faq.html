Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers about the ILMI Android App, Single National Curriculum (SNC) alignment, offline mode, and enterprise school options.

🔍

ILMI is an interactive Urdu and early literacy Android mobile application designed for children ages 4 to 7+ (covering ECCE/Nursery, Pre-K, Kindergarten, and Class 1). It uses gamification, phonics, and voice guidance to teach foundational reading, Nastaliq writing, and vocabulary.

Yes! ILMI draws directly on Pakistan's Single National Curriculum (SNC) for Urdu (ECCE – Class 1) and parallels the Common Core State Standards (CCSS ULA) for Kindergarten and Grade 1 reading foundational skills.

ILMI is built with social impact in mind. In remote or distant areas with limited internet, teachers or parents can pre-download study units via Wi-Fi. Students can then practice completely offline. Student progress data automatically syncs when connected again.

We offer dedicated B2B and institutional solutions via our Enterprise Page. Schools receive teacher dashboards, student writing portfolios, classroom progress tracking, and grade-level competency certification.

Controlled Randomness shuffles answer options, visual placements, and revision activities each time a lesson is played. This prevents children from relying on pattern memorization, ensuring genuine cognitive recall and deep mastery.

100% yes. ILMI is strictly ad-free, COPPA compliant, and GDPR compliant. We do not display third-party advertisements or track student data for commercial profiling. Read our Privacy Policy.
