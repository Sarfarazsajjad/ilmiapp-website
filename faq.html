ILMI is an interactive Urdu learning Android application designed for learners aged 3+, from early childhood through anyone who wants to learn Urdu from the very beginning. Using gamification, interactive activities, storytelling, voice guidance, and authentic Nastaliq script, ILMI makes learning Urdu letters, reading, writing, and vocabulary engaging, accessible, and enjoyable for learners of all backgrounds.

Yes! ILMI draws directly on Pakistan's Single National Curriculum (SNC) for Urdu (ECCE – Class 1) and parallels the Common Core State Standards (CCSS ULA) for Kindergarten and Grade 1 reading foundational skills.

ILMI is built with an offline-first approach, making it ideal for communities with limited internet access. An internet connection is required only for the initial Google Sign-In, profile creation, and first-time setup. After that, all lessons, stories, games, and learning activities work completely offline. Student progress is automatically saved on the device and seamlessly synchronized with secure backend servers whenever an internet connection becomes available. If the device is replaced, reset, or the learner signs in again, previously synchronized progress can be restored.

We offer dedicated B2B and institutional solutions via our Enterprise Page. Schools receive teacher dashboards, student portfolios, progress tracking, and activity records.

Controlled Randomness shuffles answer options, visual placements, and revision activities each time a lesson is played. This prevents children from relying on pattern memorization, ensuring genuine cognitive recall and deep mastery.
