Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions about Ilmi's learning methodology, pricing, curriculum, and safety.

🔍

Ilmi is an adaptive educational application designed for students in elementary, middle, and high school. It uses cognitive science techniques—such as active recall, spaced repetition, and dynamic difficulty scaling—to make studying efficient, engaging, and memorable.

Yes! Ilmi offers a generous Free Forever Tier that includes access to daily study sprints, basic flashcard decks, and core curriculum topics. Ilmi Premium unlocks unlimited offline downloads, advanced visual progress analytics, full SAT/ACT test prep suites, and family multi-profile sharing.

Our algorithm measures both accuracy and response latency. If a student answers questions quickly and accurately, Ilmi introduces higher-order problem-solving challenges. If a student struggles, the app automatically drops back to foundational principles and provides step-by-step visual explanations.

Ilmi is available on iOS (iPhones and iPads running iOS 14.0 or later) and Android (smartphones and tablets running Android 8.0 or later). You can also access web-based study decks on modern web browsers.

Yes! Students can download active study units to their device for offline study while traveling. Once the device reconnects to Wi-Fi, learning progress and streak data automatically sync with the cloud.

Student privacy is our highest priority. Ilmi is 100% ad-free and strictly compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR standards. We never sell student data or track users across third-party websites. For full details, view our Privacy Policy.

Parents can link their account to their child's profile via the Parent Portal. This allows viewing weekly mastery scores, active study streaks, total study minutes, and receiving automated weekly summary emails.

Ilmi covers K-12 subjects including Mathematics (Arithmetic to Calculus), Natural Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Computer Science & AI, Humanities, and Standardized Exam Prep (SAT, ACT, AP). See our full Curriculum Catalog.
