Designed for Scale

Enterprise Tools for Educators & Partners

From individual classrooms to nationwide literacy campaigns, ILMI provides institutional infrastructure.

📜

Single National Curriculum (SNC)

Strictly aligned with national ECCE standards and international Common Core benchmarks. Covers letter recognition (حروف تہجی), phonics, Nastaliq stroke tracing, and reading comprehension.

📡

Offline Community Support

Designed for distant, remote, and underprivileged areas with limited or no internet access. Android tablets can pre-load full study modules with periodic local sync.

👩‍🏫

Teacher Support & Progress Reports

Comprehensive student progress reports and activity insights help teachers monitor learning progress and support every child's development.

🤝

Inclusive & Adaptive Pedagogy

Controlled randomness and adaptive difficulty engine ensure first-time learners and children of diverse learning speeds progress without pattern memorization.

🌙

Urdu for a Global Audience

Designed to make Urdu accessible to learners everywhere through engaging stories, interactive activities, and authentic Nastaliq script, helping children build strong language skills regardless of their background.

Social Impact Agenda

Bridging the Educational Gap Everywhere

Our mission is to ensure quality early literacy reaches every child—regardless of socioeconomic background or geographic location.

  • Underprivileged Children: Free and subsidized digital access for community schools.
  • Remote Area Deployment: Offline Android app support without continuous Wi-Fi needs.
  • First-Time Learners: Voice guidance and visual animations for effortless onboarding.
  • National Literacy Unity: Building confident, independent mother-tongue readers.

Our Outcome Goals

1. Develop confident, independent Urdu readers and writers.

2. Equalize early learning opportunities across urban and rural schools.

3. Modernize mother-tongue education with world-class digital pedagogy.
