Urdu Curriculum & Standards Alignment

Demonstrating full coverage of Pakistan's Single National Curriculum (SNC ECCE – Class 1) and parallel Common Core State Standards (CCSS ULA) for early reading, phonics, writing, and language.

Categorized Letter Progression

Categorized Urdu Alphabet Levels (حروف تہجی)

Divided into 4 progressive segments based on complexity of shape, stroke order, curves, and dot identification.

Level 1: Simple Shapes

ا، آ، ب، پ، ت، ٹ، ث، ج، چ

Visually simple, frequently used letters that are easy to recognize, trace, and write.

  • Letter Recognition: ا، ب
  • Phonic sound: ب = بکری، ت = تتلی
  • Object tracing & stroke guides
  • Sight words: انار، انڈا، اونٹ
Level 2: Moderate Curves

ح، خ، د، ڈ، ذ، ر، س ، ش، ص

Introducing moderate curve shapes, dots recognition, and 3-letter word formations.

  • Letter Recognition: د، ر، س
  • Phonic sound: ح = حوض، خ = خربوزہ
  • Drag-and-blend CVC word builder
  • Vocabulary: درخت، رکشہ، سیب
Level 3: Loops & Dots

ض، ط، ظ، ع، غ، ف، ق، ک، گ

Focus on loops, multi-dot distinctions, ligatures, and sentence building.

  • Letter Differentiation: ض، ط/ظ
  • Ligatures & joined forms
  • Sentence builder with timed read
  • Vocabulary: عینک، غبارہ، قلم
Level 4: Complex Ligatures

ل، م، ن، و، ہ، ی ، ے

Advanced letter joining, complex Nastaliq alignment, and story comprehension.

  • Complex letter joining & tail strokes
  • Interactive story reading & questions
  • Polite expressions: سلام، شکریہ
  • Advanced vocabulary & postpositions
