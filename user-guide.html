1. Installing the ILMI Android App

The official ILMI mobile application is ready for launch on Android devices (smartphones & tablets). Follow these setup steps:

Step 1: Download from Google Play Store

Open Google Play Store on your Android device (Android OS 8.0+ supported), search for ILMI Urdu Learning App, and tap Install.

Step 2: Profile Creation

Select your user type (Student, Parent, or School Educator). Select your child's age group or class level (Nursery, Pre-K, KG, or Class 1).

Step 3: Audio & Voice Setup

Ensure your device volume is turned up to enjoy crisp audio pronunciations, phonics guidance, and character voiceovers.

💡
Family Account Tip: Parents can link up to 4 child profiles under one account to manage streaks and progress reports separately!

2. Offline Mode for Remote & Community Use

ILMI is built to serve children in distant and remote communities where Wi-Fi or cellular internet access is limited.

How Offline Pre-Loading Works

  1. Open the app while connected to Wi-Fi.
  2. Navigate to Settings ➔ Offline Content Manager.
  3. Tap "Download Full Level" to pre-load all activities, audio files, and story animations.
  4. Children can now practice completely offline! Learning metrics auto-sync when a connection is restored.

3. The 15-Minute Daily Learning Path

Lessons are organized as doorways and steps along a colorful learning path map:

  • Step 1 (Warm-Up): 3-minute quick review of previous letters.
  • Step 2 (Phonics & Tracing): Interactive Nastaliq letter tracing (حروف ٹریسنگ).
  • Step 3 (Drag-and-Blend): CVC word building with audio voiceover.
  • Step 4 (Story & Quiz): Animated storybook reading with touchable answer bubbles.

4. Parent & Teacher Progress Dashboards

Monitor child accuracy, time spent, auto-saved writing portfolios, and grade-level benchmark assessments.

Educators can access class-wide reports to identify students who need extra support on specific letter sounds or ligatures.

5. Nastaliq Penmanship Tracing Tips

For best results during letter tracing activities:

  • Use a stylus or clean index finger to follow guided direction arrows.
  • Ensure strokes start from top-to-bottom and right-to-left as indicated on screen.
  • Complete all dot placements (نقطے) to unlock star completion bonuses!
