Ilmi App User Guide

Everything you need to know about setting up your account, optimizing your daily study routine, and tracking learning progress.

1. Getting Started with Ilmi

Welcome to Ilmi! Setting up your learning profile takes less than 2 minutes. Follow these simple steps to begin:

Step 1: Download & Install

Download the official Ilmi app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Make sure your device has iOS 14.0+ or Android 8.0+ installed.

Step 2: Create a Student Profile

Select "I am a Student" during initial setup. Enter your name, current grade level (Grades 1-12), and select your primary subjects of interest.

Step 3: Take the 3-Minute Diagnostic Test

To calibrate your starting difficulty, Ilmi presents a 5-question quick quiz. Don't worry if you miss a question—the adaptive engine uses this to personalize your learning plan!

💡
Pro Tip for Parents: You can link up to 4 student profiles under a single family account to monitor each child's progress separately.

2. Optimizing Your Daily Study Routine

Consistency is the key to building strong long-term memory. Ilmi is designed for focused 15-minute daily sessions.

Recommended Daily Workflow

  1. Open Daily Dashboard: Review your active streak count and daily point goal.
  2. Complete Spaced Review: Spend 3 minutes reviewing flashcards generated by the spacing algorithm.
  3. Learn 1 New Concept: Engage with interactive visual cards explaining a new topic.
  4. Solve the Adaptive Quiz: Answer 5 practice questions to test active recall.

Streak Multipliers & Rewards

Maintaining a consecutive 7-day streak unlocks bonus gem rewards and custom avatar badges in your profile!

3. Tracking Analytics & Mastery Trees

Ilmi provides visual progress metrics so you never have to guess how prepared you are for upcoming exams.

Understanding Mastery Percentages

  • Green (80% - 100%): Mastered. Stored firmly in long-term memory.
  • Blue (50% - 79%): Learning. In active review cycle.
  • Yellow (< 50%): Needs Review. Algorithm will prioritize these questions.

Weak Spot Detection

The Analytics tab automatically highlights your "Top 3 Focus Topics" based on recent quiz response times and accuracy.

4. Parent & Teacher Controls

Parents and educators can monitor student activity without interfering with their independent learning rhythm.

Weekly Email Summaries

Parents receive an automated weekly email report showing total study hours, completed topics, streak length, and mastery progress.

Setting Healthy Screen Time Limits

In the Parent Portal, you can enable study reminders and configure daily time caps (e.g. maximum 45 minutes per day).

5. Troubleshooting & App Settings

Common solutions for app issues and offline usage:

Offline Mode Support

Ilmi allows downloading up to 5 study units for offline practice during travel. Progress syncs automatically when you reconnect to Wi-Fi.

Resetting Account Password

If you forget your PIN or password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen or email support@ilmiapp.com.
