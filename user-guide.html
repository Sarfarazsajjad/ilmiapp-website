1. Getting Started with Ilmi

Welcome aboard! Setting up your learner profile takes less than 2 minutes. Follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Download & Install

Download the official Ilmi app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store on your tablet or smartphone.

Step 2: Create Your Explorer Profile

Select "I am a Student", choose your avatar mentor (Fox, Bear, or Bunny), enter your grade level, and pick your favorite subjects.

Step 3: 3-Minute Fun Placement Quiz

To calibrate your starting difficulty, Ilmi presents a 5-question playful quiz. Don't worry if you miss a question—the adaptive engine uses this to personalize your journey!

💡
Parent Tip: You can link up to 4 child profiles under a single family account to track each child's progress separately!

2. Your 15-Minute Daily Study Routine

Consistency is the secret sauce for long-term memory! Ilmi is structured for fun daily 15-minute study sprints.

Daily Step-by-Step Routine

  1. Open Dashboard: Check your active streak counter and daily star goal.
  2. Spaced Review (3 mins): Practice flashcards generated by the spacing algorithm.
  3. New Concept Story (5 mins): Watch interactive visual cards explaining a new topic.
  4. Adaptive Quiz (5 mins): Solve 5 questions with friendly mentor hints.
  5. Collect Star Rewards (2 mins): Celebrate your streak update!

3. Skill Tree Analytics & Mastery Badges

Ilmi provides visual progress metrics so parents and kids always know what skills are mastered.

Mastery Color Indicators

  • Green Stars (80% - 100%): Fully Mastered. Stored in long-term memory.
  • Blue Badges (50% - 79%): Learning in progress.
  • Orange Dots (< 50%): Needs Review. Algorithm prioritizes practice.

4. Parent & Teacher Controls

Parents can monitor screen time, review weekly summaries, and celebrate child achievements safely.

Weekly Progress Emails

Parents receive automated weekly summaries showing study minutes, streak lengths, and mastered curriculum nodes.

Setting Healthy Time Caps

Configure study reminders and daily time caps (e.g. maximum 45 minutes) directly in the Parent Portal.

5. Troubleshooting & Support

Quick fixes for common technical questions:

Offline Mode Usage

Download up to 5 study units for offline practice while traveling. Progress syncs automatically when Wi-Fi reconnects.

Resetting Password

Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen or email support@ilmiapp.com.
