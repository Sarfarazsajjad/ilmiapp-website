Master Urdu & Early Literacy with ILMI!

The official companion website for the ILMI App. Designed for children (Ages 3+), ILMI transforms Urdu letter recognition (حروفِ تہجی), phonics, and Nastaliq stroke tracing into engaging daily learning adventures. The content is aligned with Pakistan's Single National Curriculum (SNC), supporting both the formal education system (schools and early childhood classrooms) and the non-formal education system (home learning, community education, and independent learning environments).

Why Children & Educators Thrive with ILMI

Replacing rote memorization with interactive voice guidance, engaging storytelling, fun educational activities, meaningful conversations, guided Nastaliq stroke practice, and intelligently varied learning experiences that keep every session fresh, enjoyable, and effective.

✍️

Nastaliq Letter Tracing

Guided letter tracing(حروف ٹریسنگ) to help children recognize letter shapes, correctly identify dots, and develop confident Urdu handwriting through complete letter practice.

🔊

Phonics & Voice Guidance

Audio pronunciation for letter-sound correspondences (e.g., ب = بکری), rhyme matching, and sight word fluency games.

📖

Interactive Urdu Stories

Engaging animated storybooks with touchable interactions, vocabulary context clues, comprehension activities, and interactive conversations that make reading enjoyable and meaningful.

🎲

Controlled Randomness

Dynamic shuffling of answer choices, visual placement, and revision sets to eliminate pattern memorization and foster genuine mastery.

📡

Offline Community Access

Full offline study support for children in remote areas or communities with limited internet infrastructure.

📊

Parent & Teacher Progress Tracking

Parents and teachers can track reading, tracing, activity completion, and overall learning milestones through clear progress reports inside the app.

Enterprise & NGO Solutions

Transforming Literacy in Schools & Remote Areas

ILMI provides digital learning tools for schools, teachers, NGOs, and community education programs, helping children build strong Urdu literacy skills in classrooms, homes, and low-connectivity environments across Pakistan and beyond.

  • Single National Curriculum (SNC): Aligned with Pakistan's Single National Curriculum (SNC) for primary classes, supporting both formal and non-formal education.
  • Classroom Dashboard: Teacher and administrator tools for monitoring student progress, learning activities, and overall performance.
  • Offline Tablet Hardware Sync: Designed for low-connectivity environments.
  • Offline Learning & Smart Sync: Designed for low-connectivity environments. Student progress is stored locally on the device and automatically synchronized with the server whenever an internet connection becomes available.
