Why Children & Educators Thrive with ILMI

Replacing rote memorization with interactive voice guidance, storytelling, Nastaliq stroke guides, and controlled randomness.

✍️

Nastaliq Letter Tracing

Guided stroke-by-stroke letter tracing (حروف ٹریسنگ) to build proper handwriting mechanics, letter shapes, and dots recognition.

🔊

Phonics & Voice Guidance

Audio pronunciation for letter-sound correspondences (e.g., ب = بکری), rhyme matching, and sight word fluency games.

📖

Interactive Urdu Stories

Engaging animated storybooks with touchable answers, vocabulary context clues, and polite cultural expressions (سلام، شکریہ).

🎲

Controlled Randomness

Dynamic shuffling of answer choices, visual placement, and revision sets to eliminate pattern memorization and foster genuine mastery.

📡

Offline Community Access

Full offline study support for children in remote areas or communities with limited internet infrastructure.

📊

Parent & Teacher Portfolios

Auto-saved reading and writing portfolios, progress reports, and formative feedback aligned to grade-level competencies.

Enterprise & NGO Solutions

Transforming Literacy in Schools & Remote Areas

ILMI provides institutional tools for schools, teachers, NGOs, and community programs to bridge the early literacy gap across Pakistan and beyond.

  • Single National Curriculum (SNC): Aligned for Nursery, Pre-K, KG, and Class 1.
  • Classroom Dashboard: Teacher tools for tracking student progress portfolios.
  • Offline Tablet Hardware Sync: Designed for low-connectivity environments.
  • Competency Certification: Automated benchmarks for grade-level reading goals.
