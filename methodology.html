Scientific Pedagogical Framework

The ILMI 6-Phase Progression Roadmap

Combining educational psychology, active recall, voice guidance, and controlled randomness to guide young learners from initial letter recognition to fluent Nastaliq reading and writing.

Progressive Mastery Path

6 Phases of Every Learning Module

As learners advance through the app's map, every letter and concept transitions through 6 structured phases.

1

Phase 1: Letter & Shape Recognition (حروف کی پہچان)

Learners visually identify the letter, its distinct shape, and starting stroke features through high-contrast animations.

2

Phase 2: Sound & Object Association (آواز اور تصویر)

Connects the letter with its primary phonic sound and relatable cultural objects (e.g. ب = بکری، ت = تتلی).

3

Phase 3: Visual & Dot Differentiation (حروف کا فرق)

Interactive mini-games train children to differentiate similar letter shapes, curves, and multi-dot variations (ب/پ/ت/ٹ/ث).

4

Phase 4: Nastaliq Stroke Tracing (حروف ٹریسنگ)

Guided touch-screen penmanship tracing with stroke direction arrows, alignment guides, and audio encouragement.

5

Phase 5: Cognitive Application (تخلیقی مشق)

Drag-and-blend word building, missing letter completion, rhyming bubble poppers, and sight word flashcards.

6

Phase 6: Randomized Revision (دہرائی اور مستحکم یادداشت)

Mixed reinforcement challenges combining all activity types to solidify long-term memory retrieval.

Cognitive Anti-Memorization Engine

Controlled Randomness for Genuine Mastery

Rote pattern memorization is prevented by intelligent dynamic shuffling strategy across every activity.

🔀

Answer Option Shuffling

Target letters, objects, and distraction choices are randomized on every attempt so children process phonics rather than memorizing button positions.

🎯

Dynamic Target Placement

Draggable letters and target objects appear in different visual positions on screen during each interactive exercise.

🔄

Shuffled Activity Sequence

Mid-path activity order (between steps 4 and 13) adapts dynamically based on student performance and confidence indicators.
