Delete your ILMI account

Remove your account and your children's data without installing or opening the app.

About this page

This page is for parents who signed in to ILMI with Google. It lets you delete your account and your children's data from any browser. You can also do it inside the app: Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account.

We use the sign-in below (Google) only to confirm that it is you. We do not keep anything from this page.

Before you start

  • Use the same Google account you use in ILMI.
  • School accounts cannot be deleted here. Schools delete accounts through their administrator — please contact your school.
  • If you have a subscription, it ends immediately when you delete your account: access stops now and renewal stops. Refunds are handled by Google Play, not by us. Deleting your account ends your subscription immediately and Google Play refunds your latest payment. If you have not received it or have questions, contact Google Play support.

What will be deleted

  • Your parent account and sign-in record
  • Every child profile you created
  • All learning progress for those children
  • Your consent records and emails we logged for your account

This cannot be undone. Deletion happens straight away on our servers. Your Google account itself is not affected.

If a phone still holds a child's progress, it is not changed by this page. The next time the app is opened and syncs, it finds that the account no longer exists, saves what is on the phone, and signs out.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google
  2. Check that the email shown is the right account
  3. Confirm twice
  4. Done
